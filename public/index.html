Welcome to Cinema Colosseum

You'll be shown two titles at a time. Pick the one you like more and continue to the next match up.
Your vote helps that title move up or down in rating.

To get started, in the settings card choose:

  • Genres: what you want to see
  • Popularity: how well known the titles are
  • Mode: how the game plays
  • Rounds: how many matchups you want

When you're ready, click Find Movies or Find TV Shows to begin.
