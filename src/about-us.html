About Us

Cinema Colosseum is a head to head movie and TV showdown site. Instead of rating titles with stars, you make quick choices between two contenders and those choices build a community ranking over time.

What we do

We gamify the entire movie and TV rating experience to make it more interactive and engaging for users, rather than having it feel like a chore you want to get over with. The flow of this you pick between two titles, the winner climbs, the loser drops, and the rankings get smarter as more people vote.

How it works

  • Choose your arena: Movies or TV Shows.
  • Set your match rules: adjust Settings like genre, popularity, mode, and rounds.
  • Battle: pick a winner (or skip if unfamiliar with the match up), then the next matchup loads instantly.
  • Watch rankings evolve: Leaderboards show the best titles determined by the users on this site.

Our rating philosophy

We use a competitive style rating approach: beating stronger titles matters more, underdog wins count, and rankings adjust gradually as votes come in.

Data & credits

This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.

Why we built it

Because ratings are messy and lists don't match everyone's taste. This is a faster, more fun way to discover titles and compare them.
